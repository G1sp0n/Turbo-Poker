    05Dealer cheat sheet

    The rules that cause arguments
    1. Heads-up (2 players). The dealer posts the small blind and acts first before the flop, then last on every later round.
    2. Minimum raise. A raise must be at least as big as the last bet or raise. Blinds 25/50, someone raises to 150 (+100), so the next raise is to at least 250.
    3. Short all-in. An all-in smaller than a full raise doesn't reopen betting for players who already acted. They can only call or fold.
    4. Showdown. The last player to bet or raise on the river shows first. If everyone checked, the first player left of the button shows first.
    5. Odd chip. When a split pot doesn't divide evenly, the extra chip goes to the winner closest to the left of the button. The calculator does this for you.
